Your agent can only ask.

You set one rule. The Veto program on Solana checks every request against it and pays or refuses. Your agent never holds your money.

What a rule is Who your agent may pay, the most per payment, the total it may ever spend, and when it ends. The money sits in an account only this rule uses. What a refusal is Your agent asked for something the rule does not allow, so the program said no. No money moves. The reason and time are saved on the blockchain. What the export proves The file lists every payment and refusal under a rule. Anyone can check each line against the blockchain. Show the introduction again The short tour you saw at the start.
