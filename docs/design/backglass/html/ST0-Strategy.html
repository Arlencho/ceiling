Veto, strategy board: how we make it sticky

Sticky the way a bank statement is sticky, not the way a game is.

A well-behaved agent makes a quiet week. We do not fight that. We make the glance worth five seconds, the week worth one minute, and the record worth keeping. The rule has an end date, and that clock is our retention schedule: every rule ends in a decision, and every decision starts a new record.

What we will not do
  • No streak that breaks because you did not open the app. The refusal streak counts the agent's behaviour, never your attendance.
  • No number we invented. No trust score, no rating, no stars. Only counts and amounts that are on the blockchain.
  • No daily push by default. The quiet note is off until you turn it on, at a time you pick, and refusals are the only alert that interrupts.
  • No copy that nudges spending. Never "you still have 258 to use". Left is a fact, not an invitation.
  • No points, levels, random rewards or leaderboards between owners. A plaque is a fact, not a prize.
  • No urgency at renewal and no icon badge for routine payments. If you do nothing, the rule ends and the rest returns.
How we will know it worked
  • Week in review opened within 48 hours of arriving.
  • Rules renewed within 7 days of ending, not just created.
  • Refusals seen within 15 minutes; track record cards and files shared.
  • Not measured: sessions per day. A quiet week that is trusted is a win.
