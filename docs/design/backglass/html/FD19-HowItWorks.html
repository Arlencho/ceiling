Your agent asks. The rule decides. You get told.

  1. You set the rule Who your agent may pay, how much, and until when.
  2. Your agent asks It sends a payment request. It cannot take.
  3. The program checks the rule Every request, every time, against your limits.
  4. Pays or refuses Inside the rule it pays. Outside it refuses, and no money moves.
  5. Saved on the blockchain Each payment and each refusal, with the reason.
  6. Your phone tells you A notice for every payment or refusal, if alerts are on.

What your agent can never do

  • Move money without asking
  • Pay anyone else
  • Go over the limits
  • Change the rule
